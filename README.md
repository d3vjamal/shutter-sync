# ShutterSync

**Run your photography business, not just your camera.**

ShutterSync is a business management app built specifically for photographers. From the moment a client enquires to the day you deliver — assignments, contracts, payments, and your public portfolio — everything lives in one place.

---

## Who It's For

- Freelance photographers managing multiple bookings
- Photography studios handling clients and teams
- Anyone tired of tracking shoots on paper, WhatsApp, or scattered spreadsheets

---

## What You Can Do

### Manage Your Assignments
Create detailed bookings for every shoot — client name, contact, event dates, venue, location, and exactly what you'll deliver. Track multiple shoot days for extended events like weddings. Update payment status as you receive installments and see your balance due at a glance.

Your dashboard shows everything in three clean sections: what's currently active, what's coming up, and what you've wrapped up — along with a live summary of collected revenue and pending payments.

### Generate Professional Agreement PDFs
Every assignment comes with a one-click agreement PDF you can share with your client. It includes both party details, event information, payment summary, deliverables, and the terms & conditions you've defined — all on a clean, printable document with your studio logo.

### Build Reusable Contract Terms
Write your standard terms once — cancellation policy, advance payment clause, rescheduling terms — and reuse them across any assignment. Mix and match conditions when creating a new booking.

### Showcase Your Packages
Define your service offerings with descriptions, pricing, and what's included. Mark your best-selling package as "Most Popular." These packages appear on your public profile for clients to browse before they even reach out.

### Your Public Portfolio Page
Every photographer on ShutterSync gets a shareable public page at a custom link like `yourapp.com/photographer/yourname`. It shows your bio, portfolio gallery, social media links, and service packages — a professional presence you can send to any potential client.

### Client Booking Flow
When you share an assignment link with a client, they go through a simple guided flow: they see the event details and services, read and confirm the agreement terms, then proceed to payment via UPI or WhatsApp. No login required on their end.

### Profile & Studio Branding
Set your public display name, contact details, UPI ID for payments, bio, and social handles. Upload your studio or brand logo — it appears on every agreement PDF you generate, giving your paperwork a professional, branded look.

## For Studio Owners & Admins

ShutterSync includes a separate admin panel where you can:

- Onboard new photographers to the platform
- View and manage all photographers on your roster
- Activate or deactivate accounts
- Monitor assignments and activity across the whole studio

---

## Accessing the App

| Who | How |
|-----|-----|
| Photographers | Register at the login page with your email and password, or sign in with Google |
| Clients | Access your booking link directly — no account needed |
| Studio Admins | Use the separate admin portal at `/admin` |

---

## A Note on Privacy

Client information entered into assignments (name, contact) is only visible to the photographer who created it and to the client via their unique booking link. No client data is shared publicly.
