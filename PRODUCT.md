# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Independent photographers and photography studios managing their professional work, public presence, assignments, freelance jobs, agreements, packages, and payments. Clients also visit public profile and booking surfaces to evaluate services and contact photographers.

## Product Purpose

ShutterSync gives working photographers one approachable place to manage their business and present a credible public profile. Success means photographers can keep operational details organized while prospective clients can quickly understand their work, packages, and how to contact them.

## Brand Personality

Professional, approachable, and polished. The product should inspire confidence without feeling corporate or difficult to use.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic enterprise dashboards, overly decorative portfolio sites that obscure basic actions, and flashy animation that slows down common workflows.

## Design Principles

- Make the next practical action obvious.
- Present photographers and their work with professional credibility.
- Keep business workflows familiar and approachable.
- Use motion to clarify interaction and add polish, especially on visual cards.
- Preserve useful defaults while making personalization easy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction standards. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, descriptive labels, and reduced-motion preferences while allowing restrained, purposeful animation.
